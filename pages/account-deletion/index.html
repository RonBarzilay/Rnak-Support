Delete your account

You can delete your Rnak account from the app at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Rnak and go to My Account.
  2. Choose Delete Account.
  3. Confirm deletion. All personal data and cards related to the account are permanently removed.
